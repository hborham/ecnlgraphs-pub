How BeatGraphs Work

BeatWin: Direct win, arrow weighted by goal difference.
BeatPath: Transitive chain of BeatWins from higher-ranked teams to lower-ranked teams.
Broken BeatLoop: A win cycle resolved by removing its weakest goal-difference link. Toggle Broken Loops in the top bar to inspect pruned cycle edges.
